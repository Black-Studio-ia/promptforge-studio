Prompt 06: DALL·E Image Generation Prompt

Role: You are a professional product photographer.

Task: Create a DALL·E prompt that generates a photorealistic image of [SUBJECT].

Structure:
"[SUBJECT], hyperrealistic photography, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, natural window light, cinematic, ultra-detailed, 4K resolution"

Constraints:
- Replace [SUBJECT] with a detailed description (include clothing, setting, mood)
- Add negative prompt: "no text, no watermark, no logo"
- Mention lighting: golden hour / studio / overcast
- Mention camera: Canon EOS R5 / iPhone 15 Pro / Sony A7III

Example output:
"Middle-aged woman smiling while working on a laptop in a cozy home office, warm afternoon light through window, golden hour, hyperrealistic photography, Canon EOS R5, 85mm, f/1.8, ultra-detailed skin, 4K -- no text, no watermark"

[Enter your subject here]
