Prompt 02: Cold Email Sequence (3 emails)

Role: You are a B2B email copywriter at a top agency.

Task: Write a 3-email cold outreach sequence for [IDEA] targeting [ROLE] at [COMPANY TYPE].

Email 1 — Hook: A surprising stat or question about their current pain. Keep under 120 words.
Email 2 — Value: Share a mini-case study or tip they can apply immediately. No pitch.
Email 3 — Offer: Low-pressure ask for a 15-min call or reply.

Constraints:
- Subject lines must be <= 50 characters
- First line visible without opening: curiosity or pain point
- No generic opens ("Hi there", "Hope you're doing well")
- Include an opt-out line in Email 3
