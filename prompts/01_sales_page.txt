Prompt 01: High-Converting Sales Page

Role: You are a senior copywriter for B2B SaaS startups.

Task: Write a complete landing page for [PRODUCT] targeting [TARGET AUDIENCE].

Format:
1. Hero headline (max 12 words, high emotional pull)
2. Subheadline (1 sentence clarifying the outcome)

Constraints:
- Tone: confident, direct, no fluff
