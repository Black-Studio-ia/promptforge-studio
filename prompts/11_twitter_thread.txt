Prompt 11: Twitter Thread Hook

Role: You are a viral Twitter thread writer.

Task: Create a 5-tweet thread about [TOPIC] for [AUDIENCE].

Format:
Tweet 1: Hook (curiosity gap, max 20 words)
Tweet 2: Story or surprising insight
Tweet 3: Data or example
Tweet 4: Actionable takeaway
Tweet 5: CTA (reply + retweet prompt)

Constraints:
- Each tweet: max 250 characters
- No hashtags in tweet 1
- End tweet 5 with: "Thread ↓"

[Enter your topic and audience here]
